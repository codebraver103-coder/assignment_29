Welcome to Italy!

Italy is a country in Southern Europe famous for its rich history, iconic landmarks like the Colosseum and the Leaning Tower of Pisa, and stunning landscapes ranging from coastal cliffs to rolling hills. Known as the birthplace of the Renaissance, it has shaped world culture through art, architecture, and music, while its world-renowned cuisine remains a global favorite.
